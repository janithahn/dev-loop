# Decision log

One entry per decision, newest last. A decision that changes an earlier one says so and links it.
Record it here, in the repository, not in chat or memory.

## Entry format

### D<n> - <short title> (<yyyy-mm-dd>)
- **Decided by:** <who>
- **Context:** <why it came up>
- **Decision:** <what was chosen>
- **Alternatives:** <what was considered, and why not>
- **Consequences:** <what changes, what it costs>
- **Supersedes:** <D id, or none>
